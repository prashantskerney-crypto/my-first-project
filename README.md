
# Mumbai Baarish Madad

1. Idea in a nutshell
Baarish me Mumbai me kahan paani bharega, ye pehle se batane wala AI idea. Taaki log pehle se rasta badal lein.

2. Background
Mumbai me har saal baarish me sadak pe paani bhar jata hai. Log office, school nahi pahunch paate. Gaadi band ho jaati hai.
Main Mumbai me rehta hoon. Maine khud ye takleef dekhi hai. Isliye main isko sudharna chahta hoon.

3. Data and AI techniques
Data kahan se aayega - Pichle saal kahan paani bhara tha, wo khabar se. Aur baarish kitni hogi, wo mausam wali khabar se.
AI kya karega - AI pichla paani + aaj ki baarish dekh ke bolega - "Is sadak pe paani bharega, dusra rasta lo."

4. How is it used
Ise kaun use karega - Aam aadmi jo office ja raha hai. School jaane wale bacche. Auto, taxi wale bhai.
Kahan use hoga - Mobile pe, subah ghar se nikalne se pehle - "Aaj kaunsa rasta sahi hai" - ye dekhne ke liye.

5. Challenges
Poora data milna mushkil hai. Har gali ka paani alag hota hai.

6. What next
Pehle 2-3 ilaake se shuru karenge - jaise Dadar, Andheri. Phir dheere-dheere poora Mumbai.

7. Acknowledgments
Ye mera khud ka idea hai. Kisi ka code ya photo nahi liya hai.

Aakhri baat
Mera idea chhota hai, par kaam ka hai. Agar logon ko 1 ghanta pehle pata chal jaaye ki kahan paani bharega, to bahut logon ka waqt aur Takleef bachegi.
